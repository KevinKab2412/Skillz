---
name: label
kind: concept
purpose: Organize items into overlapping categories and find them again.
familiar_as: Gmail labels or GitHub issue labels
misconception: A label shown on a group applies to every member (Gmail shows message labels on whole conversations).
op: affix l to i → find(l) includes i, until l is detached
state: labels (item → set of labels)
actions: affix · detach · find · clear
syncs: with todo (a built-in 'pending' label), with trash (a 'deleted' label)
visual: Items with tag chips; find(l) highlights the matches.
sources: Daniel Jackson, The Essence of Software (2021), ch. 6, ch. 8
origin: seed · jackson
tags: [concept]
---
