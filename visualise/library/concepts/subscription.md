---
name: subscription
kind: concept
purpose: Hear about new events from a source you chose.
familiar_as: following someone on social media
misconception: Following is mutual, or gives the follower access to private content.
op: subscribe to s → s publishes e → e appears in your feed
state: subscriptions (user → sources)
actions: subscribe · unsubscribe · publish
syncs: with notification; with post
visual: A source card with lines to subscribers; publishing sends a pulse down each line.
sources: Daniel Jackson, The Essence of Software (2021), ch. 10
origin: seed · jackson
tags: [concept]
