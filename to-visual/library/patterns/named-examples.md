---
name: named-examples
kind: pattern
purpose: Show real data next to code, so the reader doesn't simulate a computer in their head.
when: Functions whose behaviour is easier seen than read.
how: Record real calls (inputs, outputs) and name them; named examples are tests aimed at understanding (Litt's Margin Notes, 2018).
sources: Geoffrey Litt — explain-diff (https://gist.github.com/geoffreylitt/a29df1b5f9865506e8952488eac3d524), 'Understanding is the new bottleneck' (2026), 'Dynamic Documents as Personal Software' (Recurse Center)
origin: seed · litt
tags: [pattern]
