---
name: upvote
kind: concept
purpose: Rank items by community approval.
familiar_as: Reddit / Hacker News votes
misconception: Votes are unlimited or anonymous; or editing a post keeps votes earned by the old text.
op: users upvote i → i ranks higher; the same user can't vote twice
state: votes (user → item → ±1)
actions: upvote · downvote · unvote
syncs: with post (edit may reset votes); with user (no double voting)
visual: A list that reorders as votes land; a second vote by the same user bounces off.
sources: Daniel Jackson, The Essence of Software (2021), ch. 3, ch. 6
origin: seed · jackson
tags: [concept]
---
